Software Quality Engineer, 80-100% (f/m/x)

Job Description

What's it all about?

You participate in the whole development process from defining test cases up to ensuring the highest quality
You design, program and maintain multi-platform automated tests and you develop requirements for it
You execute system and verification tests along with supervising the release cycle
You extend and maintain our test and continuous integration frameworks
You continuously improve our development process, our DevOps approach and our products

What are we looking for?

You have successfully completed your education in information technology or electrical engineering (technical college or studies) and have some years of relevant working experience
You love programming and have experience in Python and/or C#
You enjoy working independently and in a structured and precise way
Linux, HIL, GIT and SSH are familiar terms for you
You are interested in complex technical systems, you pay attention to detail and you are able to think out of the box
You like to communicate in English

What makes us a great place to work?
A unique organizational culture based on trust, personal freedom and individual responsibility
A work environment with a strong focus on sustainability, diversity and flexible time management
State-of-the-art infrastructure and professional teams who enjoy their work
A creative and international environment in which innovative ideas are valued
An in-house kitchen team and many other benefits for your work/life balance
